OkeyLens — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · Version 1.0.1

TürkçeEnglish

OkeyLens is an app that recognises the tiles on your okey rack through the camera and works out what your hand scores. This document explains which data the app touches, which it does not, and what your rights are.

1. Summary

  • The camera image is processed on your device; it is not saved.
  • No account is needed; no name, e-mail, phone number, location or contacts are collected.
  • For subscribers the app works without an internet connection; on the free plan a connection is needed because the daily allowance is counted on the server.
  • There is no analytics tool and no third-party tracking code. Ads are served through Google AdMob, and Pro subscribers see none of them (see sections 6c and 12).
  • Only if you send one yourself, a limited number of screenshots are sent so that we can look into a problem (see section 6). Nothing is ever sent on its own.

2. Data controller and contact

The developer of the app and the data controller under Turkish law (KVKK no. 6698) and the GDPR is Mehmet Afif Arsay. You can write about anything in this document to mehmetarsay5@gmail.com.

3. Camera

The single purpose of the app is to recognise okey tiles in the image coming from the camera. For that we ask for the iOS camera permission; if you do not grant it the app still opens but cannot recognise anything.

Frames from the camera are processed by two machine-learning models on the device. Those models ship inside the app; they do not go online to work. Frames are not written to disk, are not added to your photo library and — unless you send a problem report yourself, see section 6 — never leave the device. When you close the app the current frame is dropped from memory.

The app does not access your photo library and does not ask for permission to.

5. Data we do not collect

Name, surname, e-mail, phone number, date of birth, location, contact list, calendar, health data, payment details, cookies and permanent hardware identifiers: none of these are collected. There is no sign-in.

The app carries two kinds of ad: a banner that sits in an empty part of the screen, and a rewarded ad you start yourself once your free scans for the day are used up. There are no app-open or interstitial ads; no ad ever interrupts the hand you are playing. Pro subscribers see no ads at all. Details are in sections 6c and 12.

6. Problem reports

The warning button in the top-right corner of the screen sends a problem report — it is how you tell us "the tiles are not being read correctly". A screenshot taken at that moment goes with the report so that we can look into it. That image contains both the scene the camera sees and what the app is showing on screen (the recognised tiles, the rack, the total); the raw camera image is not sent. In detail:

  • nothing is sent unless you tap Send; the app never sends anything on its own;
  • if no tiles are visible on screen, nothing is sent;
  • at most ten images a day, and only while connected to Wi-Fi;
  • the image that is sent is everything on screen at that moment — including whatever else is inside the camera preview;
  • the images are used to improve recognition and the app itself;
  • an anonymous installation identifier accompanies the images so that images from the same device can be told apart and the daily limit enforced. That identifier is random, is not linked to your identity or to any account, and a new one is created if you delete and reinstall the app;
  • if you send no report, no image ever leaves your device.

The legal basis is your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1, GDPR art. 6(1)(a)): you give it by tapping Send and withhold it by not sending a report. If you want an image you have already sent to be deleted, see section 10.

6b. Subscription

The OkeyLens Pro subscription is sold through the App Store. Your payment details stay with Apple; no card number, name or billing information reaches us.

To verify whether a subscription is valid and to let you restore it when you change device we use RevenueCat. What goes to RevenueCat is the App Store purchase record and an anonymous user identifier created by the app; it is not linked to your name or your Apple account.

On the free plan, so that the daily scan allowance can be counted, a counter is written to the server each time we show a result. All that is written is the anonymous installation identifier, the date and how many times it has been used that day; your tiles, your hands and your scores are not sent. The only reason the counter lives on the server is so that the device clock does not decide when the day turns over.

6c. Scans earned by watching an ad

When your free scans are used up you can, if you want to, watch a short ad and earn more scans. At most three times a day. You are free not to watch: your allowance comes back the next day, or an OkeyLens Pro subscription removes the limit altogether.

The ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, data such as your device's advertising identifier, coarse (country-level) location and device and app information reaches AdMob; that data is subject to Google's privacy policy. We neither see nor store it.

Google's server confirms to us that the ad was really watched. The only things that reach us in that confirmation are the anonymous installation identifier created by the app and the reward's transaction number; it carries nothing about your identity, the ad you watched or the advertiser.

Pro subscribers are shown no ads at all.

7. Service providers and international transfer

The frames in section 6 and the scan counter are stored on Google Firebase (okeylens-dataset); the anonymous identity token comes from Google Identity Toolkit. Subscription verification goes through RevenueCat, Inc. Ads (banner and rewarded) are served through Google AdMob (section 6c). On the Firebase side Google acts purely as a hosting provider and does not use the data for its own purposes; on the AdMob side Google's own privacy policy applies. Servers may be located outside Türkiye; by turning the setting on you also consent to that transfer (KVKK art. 9, GDPR Chapter V).

The SDKs embedded in the app are limited to these: RevenueCat (subscriptions) and Google Mobile Ads (banner and rewarded ads). Apart from those there is no other server the app connects to and no other third-party service it uses.

8. Retention

Frames that have been sent are kept as long as the recognition model is being trained and are deleted once the purpose no longer applies. The preferences on your device stay until you delete the app.

9. Security

Everything that goes over the network is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. The anonymous identity token is held in the iOS Keychain rather than in plain text and is marked so that it does not end up in device backups. The app stores no other credentials.

10. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and the GDPR you have the right to learn whether your personal data is being processed, to request information about it, to ask for it to be rectified, erased or destroyed, and to object to the processing.

If you want the frames you sent to be deleted, write to mehmetarsay5@gmail.com. Because we hold nothing that identifies you, the only way we can match your request is the anonymous installation identifier in the app; that is why you need to write before deleting the app. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

11. Children

The app is suitable for all ages (4+) and does not knowingly collect data from children. To keep children from turning on the setting in section 6, it is kept in a hidden part of the game settings page.

12. Tracking and advertising

The data we collect is not used for tracking as Apple defines it: it is not combined with data from third parties, is not used for ad targeting and is not sold to data brokers.

Google AdMob, which serves the ads, can however use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. That is why, before starting the ad SDK, the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission prompt:

  • If you do not allow it — and that is the default — your advertising identifier is never reached and ads are not personalised. No feature of the app is turned off: earning scans by watching a rewarded ad works exactly the same.
  • If you allow it, your advertising identifier is passed to AdMob and ads can be chosen according to your interests. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Either way the choice only affects advertising; nothing that goes to our own server changes.

The ads in the app are a banner and, once your free scans are used up, a rewarded ad you start yourself (section 6c). No ad covers the screen at launch or in the middle of a hand. Your game information — your tiles, your hands, your scores — never goes to the ad network in any form. Pro subscribers are shown no ads at all.

Data that Apple itself collects while you download and use the app (download statistics, and crash and usage reports if they are enabled in your device settings) is subject to Apple's privacy policy and does not reach us in a form linked to your identity.
